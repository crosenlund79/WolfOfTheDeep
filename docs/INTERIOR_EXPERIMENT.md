# Interior experiment: the cutaway boat

*Mission 004B, an experimental build for comparison with the preserved baseline.*

In this build the interior is shown as an illustrated **cutaway of the whole boat**: a side-on cross-section. The exterior, periscope, sonar, chart, combat, audio, crew, rest and cards are the same as in the baseline. Only how you see and move around the inside of the boat has changed.

## How it works

- **Keys 1, 6, 7 and 8** open the cutaway on the control room, mess, cabin or engine room.
- **The whole boat:** the patrol starts on the full cross-section. You can see every compartment and everyone in it: who is on watch, who is eating, who is asleep in the bunks.
- **Click a compartment** to go there. The view glides in, and the captain walks there. Your location still decides what you overhear and how sounds are muffled.
- **Right-click, wheel out or Backspace** returns to the whole boat. Wheel in over a compartment goes to it.
- **Click a person** to talk to them. Sleeping crew stay asleep.
- **Click a station** inside a compartment to use it:

| Compartment | Stations |
|---|---|
| Control room | periscope (3D periscope view), chart table (chart), ship control (orders) |
| Sonar room | sonar consoles (sonar display) |
| Mess | card table (cards), coffee |
| Cabin | bunk (rest), patrol log |
| Engine room | engineering panel (battery, AIP, diesels) |
| Torpedo room | tubes (status) |
| Sail | look outside (exterior view) |

- **R** toggles red lighting.
- The **depth ruler** on the left shows your depth, the ordered depth, the layer, test depth and the seabed.

## What the cutaway shows

- **Lighting:** night red in the control and sonar rooms, a red pulse at action stations, dimmer light when running silent, and the engine room flickering on the diesels.
- **People:** crew walk between stations at meal times and watch changes. Lookouts appear on the bridge when surfaced. Speech bubbles appear over whoever is talking.
- **The sea outside:** the surface and masts at periscope depth, light shafts by day, ice and seabed when close, bubbles from the screw, and snorkel exhaust.
- **Battery:** the battery cells show the charge.
- **While you rest:** the boat stays visible under a dim veil, so you watch the crew change watches while time runs fast.

## Not changed / limitations

- The old first-person 3D rooms are still in the code but not used in this build.
- Figures are stylised vector drawings with simple animation. There is no lip-sync or detailed portrait art.
- The cutaway has fixed stations. You cannot walk freely.
